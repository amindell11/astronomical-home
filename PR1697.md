Minions don't change: they fly at scale 1, so they're untouched, and their run-to-run noise is about ±15%. The probe's own `inertiaScaled` variant scales twice on this branch; ignore it.

Leftover worth knowing: the **player Valis chassis at elite scale** (`Shoal-chassis-eliteScale`) still sits near the damping limit. It shows 16 bank flips/s, bank wobble 2.7 against the minion's 1.5, and c*dt/I ≈ 1.78. The runtime factor is mass × size², and size is 1. Only the three elite prefabs got the baked 1.128² on top. That doesn't matter in game today, because only elite chassis carry an elite scale. It would matter for any future ship flown at a 2.5x torque factor on a slender Valis-family tensor.

## Flight look (films, 20 s each, elite vs a Crimson Aggressor)
Clips: `pr1697-{shoal,bastion}-elite-{realtime,slowmo}.mp4`. Realtime = 50 fps sim time. Slow-mo uses the capture's own unscaled timing: about 6.6x slow for Shoal and 3.4x for Bastion.

**Bastion (Bulwark elite):** it never settles onto the target.
- Median aim error is 30° (Shoal: 12°), and it's within 15° of the target only 29% of the time (Shoal: 58%).
- It turns hard almost constantly: 5.3 full revolutions in 20 s, median |yaw rate| 111 of a 140 deg/s cap, and 28% of the time at ≥95% of the cap.
- Distance swings 1–40 units with no stable orbit. It drifts in close (min ~1, so near-contact), throttles in reverse 34% of the time, then swings out and comes back. The orbit angle swept is 781° total but only −177° net: it rocks back and forth around the target instead of circling it.
- The aim servo never owns yaw for Bastion ships (0% for elite and minion, 0–7% for the chassis; Shoal and Veil are at 89–98%). The MPC steers yaw the whole time, which fits the slow, never-quite-pointed look.
- My hypothesis, unverified: the Bulwark's large hull radius keeps the obstacle-threat veto in YawHandover tripping on the nearby enemy, so yaw never hands to the servo. The other possibility is the AIM slot's authority never reaching the engage weight. One more logged field per tick (handover latched / vetoed / aim live) would settle it.

**Shoal (Valis elite):** stable. No flutter in the clip; it holds its nose on the target and backs off while firing (reverse 72% of the time) at about 3–11 units.
