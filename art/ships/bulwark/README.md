# Bulwark

`Bulwark.blend` retains the owner's geometry, live modifiers and organized parts. `lock.json` was refreshed with owner permission to fingerprint that geometry for texturing; flight approval remains pending. The UV-density check now passes at 1.626:1 against the 2:1 limit.

The approved symmetric plum/bone/charcoal coat remains in `layers/BaseRegion.png`, unchanged pixel for pixel. Its forward stripe stays parallel to the hood edge and crosses the nose joint. Charcoal armor and shoulder insets, plum trim and neutral metal canisters retain their palette colors. Material duplicate suffixes are now named `Secondary` for the paint tool's region schema; face assignments are unchanged.

`paint.json` uses the shared `ship_paint` workflow. `palettes.json` names every source material region in linear sRGB. `layers/Shadow.png`, `Light.png` and `Ink.png` contain generated plate outlines and tonal shapes plus authored canopy reflections. The original generation is preserved under `layers/baseline/`: never remove it. Rerunning the tool preserves the canopy edits as a difference from that baseline. Brushwork uses Vanguard's verified signed-space sampling, with a 30-degree sweep and brush contrast 3.2; no wear or chips are added. `textures/painted-brush-source.png` is the shared Crimson tile used by the sweep recipe.

Only the front and side armor PaintUV charts were separated with owner permission to remove opposite-side overlap. Other UVs and all geometry are unchanged. Both armor parts are solid charcoal, so this leaves the approved BaseRegion pixels and visible coat intact. The baseline was regenerated for the new UV fingerprint and the cockpit edits and unaffected linework were carried forward.

The active Blender materials show a flat drawn composite. Dark cockpit glass has narrow painted reflection bands, with no physical glossy reflection. The bone coat blends before shading. `Bulwark BaseRegion` remains the editable paint canvas: white adds bone, black reveals the substrate. The canopy's active paint image is `Bulwark Drawn Light`; its shadow and ink images can also be edited. Save edited images and repack them before saving the Blender source. After a tool rerun, reload and repack the three active `Bulwark Drawn` images.

Earlier shading images remain packed for preservation but are disconnected from the active output. `FinishShadow.png` and `FinishLight.png` belong to that superseded Blender preview. Current strengths and status are in `paint-settings.json`.

`reference/detail-concept.png` is the approved structural-detail direction. The clean nose keeps two fasteners; rear fins retain a single cross-panel seam. Side armor and engine shrouds carry small vent banks. These are authored edits in `Ink.png` and `Light.png`, preserved against the existing generation baseline. The base coat, asymmetric shadow mask, cockpit, geometry and UVs remain unchanged.

`layers/DetailEmission.png` is the independent light-coverage mask, packed as `Bulwark Detail Emission`. Segmented lilac rings surround metal canister centers; shoulder guards and lower skids have matching three-segment inserts. The `Detail emissive color` node controls their color and `Detail emission energy` supplies a modest 1.15 preview strength. This mask is owner-authored and separate from the painter's generated mask set: retain it and its material nodes when regenerating. It requires an emission binding alongside the pending Unity base-coat integration; it must not replace BaseRegion.

This is a Blender texture review, not Unity approval. Do not ship that incomplete bundle. The next integration must preserve the coat beneath shadow/light/ink, then obtain a fresh in-game lineup review. Flight, gameplay integration and breakup remain pending.
