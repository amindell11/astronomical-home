# Valis

`Valis.blend` is the approved editable source, on the ship-tool conventions (`art/tools/ship/README.md`): nose +Y, up +Z, mirrored across X. The root is the identity `SymmetryOrigin`. Under it, `Valis symmetry origin` keeps the approved X scale and tilt plus the 180° turn that brought the nose from -Y to +Y; it holds the 30 mesh parts under nested body and flight-surface parents, with live bilateral symmetry. Expand each parent to edit individual panels, charcoal trailing spars, tips, and pivots. The wing and aft-prong profile keys retain the original plate thickness alongside the approved tapered shapes. Role collections group the parts for export: one hull, the upper and lower wings as move roles, the fourteen approved breakup pieces as debris roles, and today's hitbox as the collider. The joined previews sit under `ignore`. `ship.json` records the exemptions the approved source keeps, and `lock.json`, written by `ship_lock`, locks the geometry. Paint is independent on the two sides of the `PaintUV` layer: the atlas's lower half carries the native charts (-X, the pilot's left) and the upper half the mirrored charts (+X, the pilot's right), so Blender's paint preview matches the game.

`layers/Shadow.png`, `Light.png`, and `Ink.png` are independent grayscale masks, packed into the Blender file. `Valis-Paint-Layers.ora` and `UV-guide.png` support hand edits. Save external image edits, repack them, and save a new Blender revision; generators must not overwrite artist edits. `paint-settings.json` records shadow 1.02, light 0.20, and ink 1.50. Base coat nodes remain independently recolorable; `palettes.json` preserves all three palettes, with jade + iris the default.

Valis is a separate playable roster entry with Ship 3's mass, engine and weapon settings. The saved Unity hull uses seven palette regions plus a contour submesh, with four rigid wing bones in one skinned renderer. Wings rest forward and sweep back over half a second under forward thrust; idle and reverse thrust share the same rest pose. The Blender source is authored in the swept flight pose. Its illustrated shader's separate-paint variant preserves damage tint and lighting; iris emission gives a small bloom through the existing gameplay profile. Palette selection follows in [#823](https://github.com/amindell11/astronomical-home/issues/823).

`brush.json` supplies the independent brush layer for the light-driven jade-iris Ivory and Cool gray materials. Run `ship_paint.py --ship art/ships/valis/ship.json --out <scratch> --brush-only` through Blender, then copy the `SHIP_BRUSH` output into `Assets/Visuals/Ships/Valis/Paint/Brush.png`, preserving its `.meta`. The export uses the approved source pose and PaintUV, so strokes meet across UV seams and stay attached during wing articulation. It leaves the three hand-edited masks and their packed Blender copies untouched.

Authoring helpers and validation tests are preserved on `codex/valis-paint-authoring-archive` at commit `2d433ca2e6790ab6f767198a59f7d6d3657405ad`. Construction experiments and review evidence live separately on `evidence/valis`.
