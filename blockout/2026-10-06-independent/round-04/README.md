# Round 04: lower tails and firmer mechanical forms

The owner rejected round 03: tails too high, missing forward pitch, oversized organic cockpit and bubble-shaped surfaces. These were base-shape errors, not details to postpone.

![Side and isometric correction](nightshade-correction.jpg)

This proposal lowers the tail tips substantially, shrinks and flattens both canopy surfaces, and replaces Catmull-Clark rounding with the editable faceted control surfaces and small chamfers. All eight X Mirror modifiers remain live. Forward pitch is interpreted as a shallow nose-down angle through the body and wings (4 degrees); that interpretation was flagged to the owner and remains subject to their feedback. The tail pair retains its bowed top silhouette with a much shallower rise.

No detail work or PR. The same editable parts can become the final model once the major shapes read correctly; this is a shape checkpoint, not a polished intermediate asset. Source check and refreshed orthographic/game-scale render sets pass. Legacy hull, integration, ShipLegacyList and breakup-slot deferral remain unchanged.
