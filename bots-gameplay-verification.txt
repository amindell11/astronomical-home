User request: full gameplay footage, bots-only, to judge the planets in context.

Setup
Real GameHost and authored ArenaSector, with two teams of four AI ships and authored respawns.
No human player rig. ObserverCam follows a live bot using its prefab's normal subject lock, zoom and velocity lead.
If the followed bot is deactivated, the capture chooses a remaining active bot. There is no authored camera path.
Normal presentation and High Fidelity quality. Planet A (gentler motion) is the committed setting in both recordings.
Three seconds of settling precede each continuous 40-second recording.
Planet surface and cloud animation remain enabled throughout.
These are spectator clips: no player-only controls, minimap or instrument HUD is manufactured.
The GIFs are silent. No art, lighting, gameplay or production source was changed for this request.

Capture and timing
Each source recording has 1002 native 1280x720 frames.
CapturePacing.Locked(2) advances .04 game seconds per recorded frame.
The simulator advances normally, while lossless frame acquisition takes longer in wall time.
The exports explicitly use 25 fps to preserve game speed; using the acquisition timestamps would slow the footage down.
GIF uses every third source frame at 960x540, with 120ms per GIF frame and 40.08 seconds total.
MP4 retains all source frames at 1280x720; the assembler adds the documented final hold.
Commands:
python scripts/capture/assemble.py <frame-directory> --fps 25 --keep-frames
python scripts/capture/assemble.py <frame-directory> --fps 25 --format gif --scale 0.75 --step 3 --keep-frames
