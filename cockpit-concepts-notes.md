# Rough cockpit concept sketches

## Sketch 1

Two heavy canopy struts converge into a narrow center projector pedestal, tying the roof, window, console, and hologram together.
The ship/detail pair sits in one low canted pod at left, while the primary/ordnance pair mirrors it at right.

Primary request: A deliberately crude ten-minute concept artist blockout of a spaceship hangar command deck/cockpit viewed from the pilot seat. Big idea: two heavy canopy struts descend diagonally from the upper window and converge into a narrow center pedestal; a small projector lens on that pedestal throws a simple glowing ship-shaped hologram into clear open air. The console, window framing, four display housings, controls, and pedestal are one continuous integrated structure.
Required layout: wide framed window onto sparse deep space across the upper middle, split by converging struts. Four blank dark-blue screens total: low left, one large chamfered trapezoid SHIP display with one smaller DETAIL display tucked below in the same canted pod; low right, one large chamfered trapezoid PRIMARY display with one smaller ORDNANCE display tucked below in the same canted pod. All four screen faces clearly visible, with no interface or text. A large warm-orange launch button near center below the hologram, plus a few chunky rectangular keys and one knob as simple blocks.

Composition/framing: exact 16:9 landscape, fixed seated eye height like current hangar, symmetrical forward view, console lower third to lower half, unobstructed hologram above console center.
