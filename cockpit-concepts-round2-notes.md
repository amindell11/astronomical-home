# Round 2 concept notes

## A — Pedestal landing (`sketch-1.png`)

From round 1 sketch 1, A takes the moderately converging canopy struts, their direct landing on the shoulders of a narrow projector pedestal, and the strong button-projector-hologram centre line.  
From round 1 sketch 2, it takes a medium-depth horseshoe dash, low sweeping screen wings, and a broad open centre pane.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: very rough 16:9 game cockpit/hangar thumbnail, variant A
Primary request: Design one different midpoint between reference round1-sketch-1 and round1-sketch-2 for a top-down space combat game's ship-and-weapons hangar. This variant is "pedestal landing": two moderately inward-leaning canopy struts descend from the upper corners and terminate directly into the shoulders of a narrow center pedestal; behind them keep a broad open central window. A medium-depth horseshoe dashboard wraps around the viewer, with low sweeping left and right wings integrated into the pedestal.
Input images: round1-sketch-1 and round1-sketch-2 are composition and exact loose marker-pencil style references; ref6-fighter-cockpit-blue is vibe and color reference; current-hangar-in-game is camera framing only and its shapes must not be copied.
Scene/backdrop: pilot-seat view through a strut-framed window onto deep blue space, stars, and a faint blue nebula.
Subject: one unified near-black blue-grey cockpit structure. Clear air at center, with a cyan wireframe fighter hologram rising unobstructed from a glowing circular projector lens on the center pedestal. Left sweeping wing contains exactly two blank cool-blue angular screens: a clearly larger SHIP screen above/outer and a smaller DETAIL screen below/inner. Right sweeping wing contains exactly two blank cool-blue angular screens, PRIMARY and ORDNANCE, as a matched equal-size pair. All four screens face the viewer enough to read. Near center foreground: one large orange launch button only, three chunky dark keys, and one round knob.
Style/medium: VERY ROUGH industrial-design thumbnail on warm off-white paper, loose dark navy marker blocks, visible pencil construction lines, scratchy overlapping strokes, uneven hand-drawn perspective, broad chisel-marker texture; same looseness and palette as the two round-1 sketches, not presentation art.
Composition/framing: single straight-on 16:9 frame from pilot seat, console across lower third, window across upper middle; strong center line; wide open center pane; wings rise only slightly toward viewer.
Color palette: near-black navy blue-grey structure, cool blue screens and tiny cyan accent lights, cyan hologram, orange exclusively on launch button.
Constraints: exactly four blank screens; no readable text, no letters, no numerals, no icons, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Console, frame, screens, controls, struts, and pedestal must read as one continuous structure. Hologram must be centered, fully visible, and unblocked.
Avoid: polished render, photorealism, 3D CGI, clean vector art, glossy materials, orange anywhere except launch button, extra screens, missing screens, floating tablets, giant UI overlays, people, hands.
```

## E — Forked spine (`sketch-5.png`)

From round 1 sketch 1, E exaggerates the inward strut convergence and turns the pedestal into a taller prow linking projector and launch button.  
From round 1 sketch 2, it takes high embracing wings and a deep horseshoe, then cuts their inner edges down so the hologram and wide centre pane stay clear.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: very rough 16:9 game cockpit/hangar thumbnail, variant E

This variant is "forked spine": two aggressively inward-leaning canopy struts descend toward the projector, then each kicks outward at the bottom and lands on a raised inner shelf of its dash wing. Between those forked feet, a taller narrow prow-like center pedestal carries the projector and continues the strong center line down to the launch button. The horseshoe is deep at the sides but cut away low around the hologram, combining embracing wings with a very open middle.
Input images: round1-sketch-1 and round1-sketch-2 are composition and exact loose marker-pencil style references; ref6-fighter-cockpit-blue is vibe and color reference; current-hangar-in-game is camera framing only and its shapes must not be copied.
Scene/backdrop: pilot-seat view through a strut-framed window onto deep blue space, stars, and a faint blue nebula.
Subject: one unified near-black blue-grey cockpit structure. Clear air at center, with a cyan wireframe fighter hologram rising unobstructed from a glowing circular projector lens on the tall center pedestal. Left rising wing contains exactly two blank cool-blue angular screens: a clearly larger panoramic SHIP screen and a much smaller clipped-corner DETAIL screen nested below it. Right rising wing contains exactly two blank cool-blue angular screens, PRIMARY and ORDNANCE, as a matched equal-size pair stacked along a single sharply swept bezel. All four screens face the viewer enough to read. At center foreground: one large orange launch button only, three chunky dark keys, and one round knob.
Style/medium: VERY ROUGH industrial-design thumbnail on warm off-white paper, loose dark navy marker blocks, visible pencil construction lines, scratchy overlapping strokes, uneven hand-drawn perspective, broad chisel-marker texture; same looseness and palette as the two round-1 sketches, not presentation art.
Composition/framing: single straight-on 16:9 frame from pilot seat; dramatic strong center axis; wide upper center pane; side wings rise prominently toward viewer while their inner edges step down to preserve clear space around hologram.

Constraints: exactly four blank screens; no readable text, no letters, no numerals, no icons, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Console, frame, screens, controls, forked struts, and pedestal must read as one continuous structure. Hologram must be centered, fully visible, and unblocked.
```
