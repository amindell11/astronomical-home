Use case: stylized-concept / sketch-to-render.
Create one high-resolution landscape spaceship concept sheet titled "BULWARK", for a powerful tank-class ramming spacecraft in the game Astronomical.

REFERENCE ROLES:
Image 1 is the owner's original pencil sketch and is the binding geometry and silhouette authority. It shows a SIDE view above and a TOP view below; in BOTH the blunt nose is at LEFT, the forked tail is at RIGHT. Interpret this sketch carefully and translate it faithfully into a polished design; do not replace its form with a generic ship.
Images 2 (Vanguard), 3 (Crimson), and 4 (Valis) are existing project ships, STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: bold hand-inked silhouettes and selectively weighted contours, broad painted light and shadow shapes, crisp simplified faceted armor, restrained mechanical panel seams, dark recessed structure and dark canopy glass. Do not copy their thin pointed fighter silhouettes, wings, or colors wholesale.

BULWARK FORM:
Preserve the lower sketch's wide, solid shield/arrowhead body, flat blunt front ramming edge, broad chamfered front shoulders, central inset teardrop cockpit oriented toward the blunt prow, tapered waist aft, two separated substantial trailing fins/engine prongs, and the negative space between those aft prongs. The front is a single broad physical armored ram, NOT a long narrow beak, not two long forward tusks. Keep the sketch's short broad stance and longitudinal proportions, not a long fighter. In SIDE view preserve the low squat wedge, sloping forward deck, armored lower edge, long flat back sloping toward the split rear, and the large circular side inset underneath/abeam of the cockpit area. Treat the circle as an armored mechanical side pod, not a wheel; no wheels or tracks. Give the ram a thick layered structural edge, making the ramming role legible within the given silhouette. Rear fins have a short raised terminal profile from the sketch, not enormous wings. Keep surface detail sparse and purposeful, large uninterrupted armor faces, small vents in recesses. Bilateral symmetry. No external gun barrels or turrets: they would alter the requested design.

SHEET:
One consistent ship design across four generous views: large orthographic TOP (nose up is allowed, equivalent to rotating sketch clockwise), clear orthographic SIDE (nose left), FRONT showing the wide low ram and recessed cockpit, and a large FRONT THREE-QUARTER hero view. All four must agree exactly on proportions, cockpit, side pod placement and aft split. Top + hero occupy most of the page; side and front remain clearly readable. Add one small top-view silhouette/readability thumbnail. Flat muted slate background, careful spacing, clean minimal labels "TOP", "SIDE", "FRONT", "THREE-QUARTER". No decorative layout machinery, no environment, no combat, no other ships, no notebook lines.

FINISH:
Match the project reference drawings, particularly Crimson's broad matte painted shadow shapes and Vanguard's clear layered panel construction. Restrained palette related to the reference fleet: neutral warm silver/ivory armor, dark charcoal structure, a few warm muted orange inset accents, dark blue-gray canopy and very small cool engine lights. Palette is an initial proposal, not extra emblems or lore. The silhouette and relative masses from the owner's sketch take precedence over style or detailing. Readable in a top-down 2.5D game at small size. Solid heavyweight spacecraft, not a ground tank, not photoreal, not shiny showroom rendering, no micro-greeble noise.
