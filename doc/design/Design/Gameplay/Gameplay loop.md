1. Begin in [[Home Base]].
2. Respawn as a clone and talk to your AI companion and other base characters.
3. Assemble a ship using parts acquired on previous runs. Different combinations determine stats and abilities.
4. Embark on a run, traveling from [[Sectors|sector]] to sector. Each sector contains several systems/scenarios, each with its own goals and challenges.
5. Complete a scenario to earn rewards (ship parts, weapons, upgrades, cash/resources).
6. Complete a sector, then upgrade your ship before moving to the next sector.
7. Progress toward the larger run goal (TBD).

Notes:
- Sector map is not linear. You can traverse sectors in any order.
- Some sectors are harder than others; waiting can be safer, but early risk can yield higher rewards.
