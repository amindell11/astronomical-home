Think [[Hades]] or FTL.

Each run, you travel through a series of rooms/levels, gaining upgrades and power as you go, progressing toward a final goal or boss.

Each room is different, and there are also different sectors (groups of rooms you travel through) with different environments and atmospheres.

If you die, you lose everything gained on that run and start from scratch, but you may also gain permanent rewards/upgrades along the way that persist.

## Sector presentation (WIP)
Sectors may be presented as either:
- a classic **node map** (FTL / Slay the Spire), or
- a small **open-space overworld** with POIs (see [[Sectors]])

Likely direction: a hybrid where you physically fly between nearby POIs (keeps immersion), while pacing/content remain manageable for a small team.
