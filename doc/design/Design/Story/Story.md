You are a clone trying to find out why you were made.

Each time you die, you respawn as a new clone, but your memories are backed up and restored from your previous version.

Your creator is nowhere to be found. One story-driven goal is to discover why they made you and what happened to them.

You are accompanied by an AI companion who can give gameplay tips and story reactions. Their memory has also been wiped, so they cannot tell you more than you know about your past.

Your creator did leave you one clue as to where he went (TBD) this is the center of the story and of your mission. you travel to various sectors collecting information and artifacts in order to eventually locate your creator and ask him one question: why?? why create you and then leave? 

There are 3-5 other characters you meet along the way: some fly missions with you, others are met in base.
