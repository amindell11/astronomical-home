# Encounters

Encounters are discrete events/objectives that happen within a [[Sectors|Sector]]. They are defined primarily by **objective**, not by “combat vs non-combat”.

Design inspiration: [[GDC - Designing Radically Non-Linear Single Player Levels | Nonlinear Levels talk]]

## Encounter taxonomy (objective-first)

### 1) Combat: Skirmish
- Objective: win the fight or disengage.
- Common use: resource drain, pacing, optional rewards.

### 2) Combat: Elite
- Objective: defeat/disable a stronger target.
- Can also serve as a “gatekeeper” to an objective, or a high-risk/high-reward optional challenge.

### 3) Objective missions (combat can be pressure)
These are “win by doing the thing”, with combat/hazards as complications.
- **Scan / investigate** (derelict, anomaly, beacon)
- **Hack / disable** (jammers, relays, turrets)
- **Defend / hold** an objective while a timer runs
- **Escort** a weak ship through danger (asteroid field / gravity anomaly / red zone / solar flare)

### 4) Hazard / navigation
- Objective: traverse a hazardous region or reach a point under navigation constraints.
- Examples: asteroid fields, mine/debris zones, gravity anomalies, solar flares.

### 5) Opportunity / resource
- Objective: take a calculated risk for optional rewards.
- Examples: loot cache, salvage pocket, shop-like event.

### 6) Narrative choice / information gathering
- Objective: learn what happened / locate someone or something / make a choice.
- Often functions as a **lead** (unlocks or points toward a larger objective).

### 7) Sector Objective (Key acquisition)
The “key” is the sector intel/artifact needed to progress the broader run goal.

Key acquisition principles
- Key acquisition should be **recoverable** (players can retreat and try again).
- Key acquisition can be tied to:
  - an objective location/event (derelict scan/salvage/defend, hack, etc.), or
  - a target (e.g., key carrier) that may attempt to escape.

### 8) Extraction Challenge
Extraction is the act of leaving a sector.
- Extraction should make **flee/extract meaningful** (not “kill everything”).
- Sectors can gate extraction behind an **Extraction Challenge** after the player completes the sector objective.

Common extraction challenge modes
- **Hazard navigation** (e.g., asteroid field)
- **Escape while being hunted** (pursuit/interdiction)
- Combination variants are allowed.

### 9) Wingman (sector ally)
Some encounters can grant a **wingman** who follows and assists the player.
- Typical sources:
  - rescue a ship in distress
  - complete an escort/defense objective
  - narrative choice / faction favor event
- Wingman is an optional power spike that can influence later fights and extraction.

## Open questions
