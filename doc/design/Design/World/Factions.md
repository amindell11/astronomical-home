# Factions

Three factions, one per sector: [[Sectors]]. The clone story ([[Story]]) waits for M2; in M1 the world is told through the companion's lines.

The factions carry the theme from [[Themes and Motivation]] (greatness vs. enjoying the little things): the Shoal live it, the Bastion and the Veil have forgotten it.

From flying: enemy ships do have to be a lot weaker than me, especially as more come, and there's no way around it, but it can be built into the lore. Each faction needs its own reason one ship is weaker than the player.

## Shoal

Salvage clans. Fast scavengers. Can't afford better ships. See your ship as a scrap opportunity. Often they just pick at the wrecks of long-lost battles. Some of them are veterans.

- **Want:** build a rich and wholesome life for their kids. They're simple folk. Think Firefly.
- **Fear:** they fought and continue to fight only out of necessity, and they fear this will end in inevitable conflict. They just want to live in peace with their own.
- **What they're right about:** stopping to enjoy the little things.
- **Split:** some think they should just make do with what little they can mine instead of putting themselves at risk by raiding Bastion ships. Many have also been lost to the Veil traveling through unknown space.
- **Off duty:** they have space pets (like alien cats, and maybe some other weird aliens), they mine, and they have their own music style and games they like to play.
- **Voice:** almost like a space country accent, something like Han Solo.

## Veil

A secretive order of outlaws who prey on unsuspecting ships that pass through unknown space. Kind of like technology accelerator people. The Bastion is conservative and wary of technological advancement, so the Veil began as an underground tech black market (Blade Runner style) for robots and advanced stealth tech. They have paid the price for this by sacrificing some of their humanity.

They can out-maneuver anyone with their superior technology and stealth. They live plugged into their ships and eating from a tube, in a virtual world (like the humans in the Matrix).

Who's really in charge, the plugged-in humans or the robots? Nobody knows. Their ships are disposable, and it's unclear if they even have people in them. Are they people flying disposable drones, or drones flying disposable people?

Why do they even engage in reality? The robots are hostile and want to assimilate those passing through. People think they are biding their time and amassing numbers to launch an all-out assault on humanity.

- **Want:** a world where humans and robots are one being, unified for the purpose of efficiency. Strive to optimize everything.
- **Fear:** failure, meaninglessness, and chaos destroying the machine they have fought so hard to build. They are ruthlessly protective of it.
- **What they're right about:** they understand the unique power of humans augmented by technology better than anyone.
- **Split:** nobody knows whether the humans or the robots are in charge.
- **Off duty:** plugged in, in a virtual world. Little time for humor (it's inefficient).
- **Voice:** robotic, clipped.

## Bastion

Militaristic and honorable, but more like Mandalorians than an organized army. Stubborn for sure, and headstrong. Proud warriors who sometimes overreach and try to subjugate other cultures in the name of order.

- **Want:** hierarchy and meritocracy, with loyalty and heart. No alien cats, and definitely no robot-human relationships: they have banned robots completely.
- **Fear:** the unknown. Technology taking over, aliens. They just want to stick to their ways and have things work in a simple way.
- **What they're right about:** they have their humanity. And their boldness and initiative keep them safe and well resourced, whereas the Shoal struggle to stay afloat and make ends meet.
- **Split:** the clans all think they are the strongest, best and bravest, so they trip over themselves trying to outdo each other and hold deep-seated grudges.
- **Off duty:** weapons and duty are their lives. They spend their free time polishing their weapons, dueling, and playing deathsports.
- **Voice:** military, cocky, headstrong, bullish.

## Between factions

- The Bastion have made efforts to clear out the Veil, but the Veil are slippery and elusive.
- There is no alliance between the Shoal and the Veil.

## The companion

A good robot. Remembers the factions and general information, not his own past. Makes comments about the sector you're in, the ships you're facing, or the ship that killed you. He knows the Veil's robots are evil and have immoral views about how to deal with humans.

## The wider world

No intelligent alien factions, but there is alien wildlife. Maybe hint that people may have had sparks of alien interaction, or rumors of it.
