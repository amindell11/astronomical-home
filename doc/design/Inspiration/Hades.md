Love the balance of upgrades that you get along the course of a run as well as over the span of many runs.  (unlock scoreboard, weapons, etc). 

The diversity of weapons, boons, keepsakes, and aspects which can all be combined for different synergies create an infinite number of combinations that keep the gameplay experience feeling fresh over hundreds of runs

The story works in a non-linear path, with different characters throughout the world as well as your home base revealing bits of lore as you go.

The visuals are colorful, unique, and exciting. and I love that they look kind of hand drawn
