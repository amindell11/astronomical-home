Astronomical is a 2.5D, top-down [[Rogue-like]] space shooter where players travel around the [[World]], collect loot, complete quests, and hunt for ancient secrets.

The ultimate goal of the game is TBD.

## Candidate direction, unruled (2026-11-25)

Owner evidence from flying M1 points at a core fantasy to test. The player is
outnumbered and invents a way out by rearranging the geometry of the fight,
using momentum, the ship's own body, its tools and the terrain. A creative
maneuver here means a solution the player invents during a fight. It is not a
combo the game taught and not a build assembled before the fight. This is a
candidate, not a ruling. The pillars below stay in force until the owner
rules. [[Combat]] carries the flying evidence and the draft tests for this
candidate.

Sectors are currently planned as mall **open-space worlds with POIs** (stores, encounters, activities) and limited organic encounters in transit (see [[Sectors]]).

Core pillars:
- High skill-ceiling [[Combat]].
- [[Loot and Rewards]] collected over time and per run.
- [[LLM driven AI dialogue]].

Game is built in Unity.
