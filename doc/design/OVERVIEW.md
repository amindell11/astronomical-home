Astronomical is a 2.5D, top-down [[Rogue-like]] space shooter where players travel around the [[World]], collect loot, complete quests, and hunt for ancient secrets.

The ultimate goal of the game is TBD.

Sectors are currently planned as mall **open-space worlds with POIs** (stores, encounters, activities) and limited organic encounters in transit (see [[Sectors]]).

Core pillars:
- High skill-ceiling [[Combat]].
- [[Loot and Rewards]] collected over time and per run.
- [[LLM driven AI dialogue]].

Game is built in Unity.
