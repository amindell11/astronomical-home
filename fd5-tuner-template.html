Faction Dress Tuner

The repair dock, mod station and armory in each faction's dress. Turn a knob and the stations redraw over a real game capture at true screen size. When it looks right, copy the settings block and paste it back to Claude.

The canvas is a 1280 by 720 screen. Closest zoom is the ship parked at the station; widest is the camera pulled back for a fight. "All four" shows every dress of one station side by side, each in a quarter of the screen. The ship and rocks belong to the capture and stay at closest-zoom size. Shading, hatching and outline are a Blender preview, not the game's shaders.
