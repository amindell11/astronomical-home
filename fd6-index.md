## Bastion, round 5 (option C)

| Role | Colour | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Main plates | Cream bone `#EBCFA4` | Most of the hull |
| Second plates | Aged bone `#BFA57C` | Alternate plates |
| Frame | Near black `#241F22` | Joints and the core |
| Main accent | Crimson `#8A2A38` | Armour, banners, chevron plates, accent panels |
| Second accent | Deep crimson `#6A1E2A` | Magazine caps and patch panels, and the stripes and chevrons on the armour and banners |
| Third accent | Darkest crimson `#561A26` | Panel arrays and patches |

Windows stay cold white `#D7E6FF`.

## Options considered

- **A: bone with red only on the main accent.** The second accent was bone, so stripes read bone on red, as on the proposed ships. But the armory lost nearly all its red, because the same role paints its magazine caps.
- **B: bone, with the second plates also red.** It came out nearly the same as A, since few parts use the second plate role.
- **C, picked: the second accent in deep crimson.** Red shows on every station. The cost is that stripes and chevrons are now dark red on red rather than bone on red. Separating the two would need a new colour role in the station builder.

## What the pictures are and are not

- These are Blender masks recoloured over game captures, not the game's renderer.
- Bastion's repair dock is the closest Bastion station to neutral. Its ring is mostly plates, so its red sits on the pod and hub, and its cold white windows set it apart.
- Nothing in the game has changed.
