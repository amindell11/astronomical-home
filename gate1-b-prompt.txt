DISTANCE READABILITY FIRST. Design for a top-down 2.5D space game: the complete 29-unit-wide outpost must be recognizable from a thumbnail roughly 120 pixels wide; a player's ship is about 1/15 its width. Silhouette, a generous open fly-through berth, and three unmistakably different service modules must survive reduction, even with ALL lamps and windows off.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a finished hand-drawn concept direction sheet, landscape, high resolution, on dark charcoal-indigo space. This is gate 1 concept art only, not a render, blueprint or game capture.
References, roles only:
Image 1, 01-ink-finish.png: the chosen ink finish, selectively heavy contours, dimensional simplified shading, broad panels and restrained purposeful marks. Borrow this finish and the sculptural broad shape language; do not copy the ship or console.
Image 2, 02-lighting-mood.jpg: the lighting target, warm light from inhabited openings spilling on dark warm neutral hardware. Borrow glow hierarchy, not composition or UI.
Image 3, 03-owner-cockpit.png: owner's mood reference, a lived-in place with little ambient light and warm small practical indicators. Do not copy people, movie imagery, fine switches or text.
Image 4, 04-current-repair.png: today's distant station as a scale/readability and lit-versus-spent comparator only. Replace its thin dense scaffolding completely.

Established direction: independent neutral crews run small manned harbour outposts in hostile open space. Welcoming, adventurous, expressive, patched by practical repairs. Neutral but not monochromatic: dark warm graphite, tobacco brown, mushroom taupe, oxidized pewter; several large warm-neutral color blocks. A few broad patch plates, simple pipe groups, housing seams and windows that explain actual inhabited rooms. Bold confident ink, variable contour weight, a few decisive light and shadow planes, restrained local bloom. Rich silhouette and ambitious character are welcome. No dense greebles, checker panels, white frosty hull, glossy photorealism, faction paint, flags, hazard diamonds, big UI emblems, medical cross, gun icon or portal energy membrane. Empty space must visibly continue through the central berth. No solid floor or back wall through the fly-in channel. No wispy wire cage.

Shared kit: one hull per direction repeated identically for REPAIR, CACHE, ARMORY, swapping only its service module and service lamp. Keep the module on an outside rim and leave the berth clear. Repair = conspicuous paired curved service arms with broad tool pads. Cache = one chunky strapped supply drum/cassette with lid open and a single broad load visible. Armory = three tall flat equipment lockers fanned outward like a comb with three selectable bays, not giant guns. These shapes must differ even when unlit.
Light proposals, colored light only, no colored faction hulls: REPAIR pale straw-yellow #DDD68A, CACHE pale dusty peach #D6AE93, ARMORY low-saturation steel-periwinkle #9AAFD6. All types also have a SMALL shared warm amber crew-window strip. The repair light is deliberately not green; the old green risks Veil lime. No saturated orange, acid lime, cyan, teal, red, magenta, violet or mint station glow. Type identity comes primarily from module silhouette and secondarily from hue, not a color-only test.

Sheet layout: clear sparse typography, generous margins. Header has the letter and direction name. Three aligned columns labelled REPAIR, CACHE, ARMORY. Top row gives a LARGE inviting lit three-quarter view of each complete station, same angle and scale. Second row gives each SAME full station LARGE ENOUGH TO READ, all windows black and ALL service lights, berth lamps and emission OFF, under faint neutral ambient light so silhouette and ink remain visible; label this row DARK. Do not show any colored glow in dark states. A narrow bottom strip for each column shows a small near-top-down LIT and DARK pair, each station about 100-130 pixels wide, with generous dark space around it and one tiny gray player ship for scale. Label the strip DISTANCE STUDY. An inset at bottom edge shows the bare SHARED HULL top view, side view and front view, no modules, simple small drawings that explain the open volume. Title footer CONCEPT ONLY. Do not label simulated pixels as an engine test. The large versions show volume and real separable parts, not flat graphic icons.

DIRECTION B: BROKEN-RING ROADHOUSE. A thick, faceted crescent around an enormous open center, with a broad missing lower-right quarter as an entry, not a closed ring. The crescent is low and horizontally oriented in the game's flight plane, with its two ends blunt and clearly separated. A squat offset crew house perches on the thick outer rear arc with two small stacked rows of warm windows, like a roadside diner adapted to space. Asymmetric patched broad cladding and one short utility mast make it inhabited, whimsical and sturdy. The module attaches to the outer left shoulder, leaving the whole interior and gap empty. Not an upright portal, ceremonial arch or extraction gate; it is a working circular dock. The silhouette must remain unmistakably a broken chunky circular harbour even in darkness.
