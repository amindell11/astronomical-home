## What needs correction before concept acceptance?

- A has residual amber light on an inner berth edge in the large dark view. Cache and armory service hues are not carried onto the object clearly.
- B shows the clearest full shutdown. Its ring may read too similarly to the planned extraction structure. The shared-hull top inset drifts from the hero's opening.
- C leaves blue armory lamp panels on in its large dark view. The distance armory goes dark. The top and front insets are exploratory, not a build specification.
- Distance strips are approximate readability studies. Their ship figures are oversized. They do not establish world dimensions or an actual camera-distance test.
- All three use a very warm overall light. The straw repair and peach cache lamps need more separation. The steel-periwinkle armory needs review against Bastion cyan and find violet at game distance.
- Some panel seams read as masonry. The chosen direction should retain bold ink while making its patchwork read as metal.
- The side/front/top insets are exploratory. Exact shared geometry needs an agreed turnaround before modelling.

## Which service colours are proposed?

Repair uses pale straw-yellow #DDD68A. Cache uses pale dusty peach #D6AE93. Armory uses low-saturation steel-periwinkle #9AAFD6. Small amber crew windows belong to the common hull. These values describe the prompts, not measured pixels or an accepted palette.

The current repair green risks confusion with Veil lime. Straw and peach need care around Shoal orange and the amber lull beacon; the pale armory blue needs care around Bastion cyan and minimap violet. The module shapes carry identity when the lights are off. No colour is ruled by this round.

## What does this evidence establish?

Each sheet explores one shared hull with repair, cache and armory modules, large lit/dark views, small distance views and hull-volume insets. These are image-model concepts only. Gate 4 owns the in-engine comparison, all service states, two locales, asset outlines and mesh counts.

Reference images remain outside this evidence publication. Each provenance sidecar records their roles and pinned source links. No primary-tree files were written. No Blender blockout or game integration was started.
