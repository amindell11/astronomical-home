TITLE: "CACHE / SPLIT CARGO POD"
DESIGN: Invent a distinct floating storage outpost shaped like a SHORT FAT faceted egg opened into THREE thick curved hull leaves. Each leaf is a closed habitable volume with a rounded outer back, a recessed inner cargo surface and a long simple rib following its bend. The three leaves wrap around a generous EMPTY central chamber in three radial orientations 120 degrees apart. One leaf is slightly shorter and one is displaced along the long axis, making its three-dimensional assembly readable. Total longest dimension only 1.25 times its width: compact seedlike mass, not an elongated ship. A few short stout curved couplings at one shoulder hold the leaves together while keeping wide gaps between them and access through both axial ends. This is an open pod of habitable modules, not a closed sphere or merely a ring cut into three segments. Cargo cassettes are nested into inner leaf surfaces at different orientations, not stacked on a bottom. The lid of a terrestrial chest is absent. Both outside and inside have recessed warm crew windows and broad service sockets which wrap around their curvature. No windows arranged in one top row. Warm amber crew light, a few pale dusty-peach cargo apertures; no neon orange. The reverse view should show the curved backs as fully designed machinery with windows and couplings; the edge view still reads a chunky split pod with visible open space. Draw one object across all views, not a group of unconnected pods. It must look nothing like the repair C ring or the armory's rectilinear rack.
