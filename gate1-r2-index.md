The structures share materials, inhabited light and drawn finish. They no longer depend on a single shared hull with attachments. The split capsule follows an initial cache study that still read as a segmented ring. Both were shown without manual retouching and are preserved.

## What changed in the construction?

The repair ring has a rounded hull cross-section with windows, pipes and service fittings continuing around the back. The cache has thick opposing curved housings joined at one end, with exposed supplies facing inward. The armory has thick equipment bays and connectors arranged in depth, with a compact crew capsule integrated into the assembly. No design has a ground plane, feet, a flat foundation or a house perched on a deck.

Each sheet includes tilted, reverse, edge, dark and small views. Reverse surfaces are part of the design. The dark views turn off the principal crew and service lights.

## What still needs judgement?

The owner needs to judge whether these feel free-floating and distinct enough. The cache capsule's intended off-axis twist is mild, and several small views repeat similar attitudes. The armory's central opening is narrow from some views. Its rear has broad closed panels while its front has open equipment bays; the difference is functional, but its appearance under rotation remains for the owner to judge.

These image-model drawings do not prove a consistent 3D turnaround, arbitrary rotation in motion or the actual play camera. Small views are not calibrated distance measurements. Light colours remain proposals from the prior round, with repair green versus Veil lime and the other palette collisions still unresolved by an in-engine test.

## How was this made?

The built-in image model made all four sheets. No manual image edits were applied. The three initial sheets reference the first-round broken-ring image, the chosen console ink finish and the console lighting study. Each prompt labels the references' roles. The capsule follow-up uses the first round-two cache sheet as its sole edit target and asks to change geometry only.

Image hashes are in [the hash record](gate1-r2-sha256.json). All work and the publication checkout remain outside the primary tree. No Blender, Unity or integration work has started.
