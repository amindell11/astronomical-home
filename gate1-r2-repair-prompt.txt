TITLE: "REPAIR / BROKEN RING"
DESIGN: Preserve the appealing broken-circle silhouette, but rebuild it as a chunky inhabited broken TORUS. A torus is a tube curved into a ring; its tube has an approximately rounded hexagonal cross-section, not a rectangular brick. Ring outside span about 29 units, generous central aperture about 16 units across, inhabited tube about 6 units thick. The tube undulates slightly forward/back through space, with one end advanced and one end recessed; almost a loose C-shaped spiral but not a thin spring. Missing quarter opens onto space. Rounded broad endcaps and hollow service recesses at both tips have thick handles and wraparound emitters. Crew windows are staggered around the tube's INNER AND OUTER circumference and around its reverse, fully integrated into the hull. No top-only cabin. A few independent curved repair tools wrap around the inner tube on alternating sides, rather than sitting on a flat deck. Tool sockets remain legible from either side; each end has one broad working paddle. Recessed crew windows warm amber, service light pale straw-yellow, no green. The edge view must show an inhabited tube thickness around a quarter of the full span, not a razor-thin C. Reverse-side construction is deliberately as rich as the hero. This structure is all curve and negative space, distinct from the cache's split capsule and armory's angular rack.
