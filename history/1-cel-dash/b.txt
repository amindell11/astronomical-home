Launch (continued) and Trail: instead of ghost copies, sharp cel-banded SPEED STREAKS peel off the ship's wingtips, pod edges and tail and stream straight backwards along its path: long thin jagged blades, white at the root, cyan, then deep blue at the tips, five to seven of them, the outermost from the wingtips longest. In Trail the streaks have detached from the hull and are breaking into short shards that drift backward and shrink; the exhaust spike has shrunk.
Settle: the ship cruises with normal small engine flames; a few last cyan shards fading far behind.
