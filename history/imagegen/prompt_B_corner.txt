A sheet of seven HUD weapon glyphs for a space-combat game. Flat, very dark navy background (#0a1020). One row of seven equal square cells, each glyph large and centred in its cell, with a small off-white caption under each, in this order: LASERS, TURBO LASERS, CHARGE LASERS, RAILGUN, MISSILES, RIPPERS, CONCUSSION.

Rules: all slashes share one steep axis about 63 degrees from horizontal; one stroke weight throughout; flat, no glow, no gradients, no circles, no soft curves; no literal guns or missile silhouettes; the seven must read as one family built from the same construction, yet each distinct in silhouette and guessable at a glance; readable when shrunk to 24 pixels.
