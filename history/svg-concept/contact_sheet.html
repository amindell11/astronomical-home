Laser bolt: layer concept

Hard-banded cel core with jagged electric edges, plus one soft bloom. Travel direction →. Fringe and glow are additive (screen).

Layers (blue)

1 · body: rim / mid / hot / core bands, jagged edges
2 · electric fringe: crackle filaments (additive)
3 · glow: the only soft layer (additive)
4 · muzzle flash
5 · impact burst + shock ring
6 · impact spark (mini streak)

Composite, 4 frames flickering (body + fringe swap every ~50 ms)
