Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: exploratory concept sheet of seven tiny weapon-identification icons for a sophisticated science-fiction game's ship health-bar UI.
Input Image 1 is a reference to the previous rejected concept direction, NOT an edit target and NOT an approved design. The user wants much cleaner and even more minimalist designs starting from this idea. Redesign them, do not just thin the old shapes. Keep the restrained ivory-on-deep-navy palette and otherworldly spacecraft-instrument feeling.
Make a polished flat 2D icon-design sheet. Exactly SEVEN icons, equal optical size, arranged four across on top and three centered below, with ample negative space. Each icon occupies a roughly square footprint. ONE icon per weapon, no variants or duplicates within this sheet. Small understated sans-serif labels below: top row "LASERS", "TURBO LASERS", "CHARGE LASERS", "RAILGUN"; bottom row "MISSILES", "RIPPERS", "CONCUSSION". No other text. Keep every icon wholly inside its allotted space.
Thin but clearly visible off-white strokes, crisp smooth vector-like edges, uniform line weight across all symbols, all shapes solid flat off-white. No texture, no distressed edges, no shading, no glow, no gradients, no 3D. No card frames, no cell borders, no ornate circuitry, no decorative specks, no surrounding stars. Symbols must be strikingly simple and plausibly legible at 24–32 pixels. Aim for 2–4 meaningful elements per icon, no tiny ticks or micro-details. Give each weapon a different silhouette or arrangement; avoid seven variations of the same slash. The three laser variants should share a family resemblance.
Weapon meanings: Lasers are repeating straight energy bolts; Turbo Lasers are faster weaker bolts; Charge Lasers accumulate energy before a stronger shot; Railgun is a precise charged instant hit; Missiles pursue a target; Rippers fire a straight sequence of physical slugs, not a shotgun spread; Concussion expands outward in a blast.
Avoid literal guns, literal missiles, arrows/arrowheads, crosshairs, heraldic shapes, familiar app icons, and letters used as symbols. Evoke the firing behavior with abstract geometry. These should feel like quiet future instruments, not tribal tattoos or fantasy runes.
Direction B: INTERRUPTED INSTRUMENTS. Explore a meaningfully different family, using short open circular arcs combined with one or two offset straight marks. Mostly open space, no enclosing rings around every icon, no generic reticle motif. Precise monoline strokes with flat ends, extremely restrained. For the laser family use a common straight beam element and distinguish repetition, fast cadence and stored charge with minimal geometric changes. Railgun should have a unique long precise axis. Missiles should evoke pursuit through an offset dot and a partial curved path, no arrowhead. Rippers should evoke three discrete slugs in an ordered sequence. Concussion should be a few detached outward arcs. Maximum four elements in any symbol, no micro-ornaments. The result should be lighter, quieter and more alien than the reference, with the clarity of an actual instrument symbol system.
