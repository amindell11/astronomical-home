# Projected paint masks, retired 2026-10-17

The Shadow, Light, Ink and Emission masks Nightshade carried on main until the `ship_paint`
conversion (#868, owner ruling of 2026-10-14: every ship converts onto the recipe). They were
derived by projecting two imagegen marker paintings onto the mesh and decomposing them per
material (texture repair 05), against the dark concept-05 palette, and were never re-derived
after the owner recoloured to pale blue. Kept here with the `.ora` that exposed the layers,
the `paint-settings.json` they were tuned with, and the `palettes.json` of the day (regions
named without the slot prefix, plus a `Green` accent entry that was not a slot).

Nothing here is an input to the recipe; the tool regenerates masks from the locked geometry.
