## Why was the reused set rejected?

On November 7, 2026, the user rejected the sixty-variant reuse exploration and asked to return to a few unique asteroids per locale. The old rounded source bodies still dominated the results, and several material treatments read as additions to those bodies. The result did not meet the approved Violet's visual quality. Technical fit checks and a complete catalog did not establish that aesthetic quality.

Stop the expanded reuse pass. Preserve its saved files and review images as rejected exploration. No runtime integration, export, merge or further cleanup of that set is requested.

## What is the revised quantity and approach?

The user explicitly chose five unique asteroids per locale, twenty-five total. The approved Violet prototype counts as one of the five Violet asteroids and remains unchanged. This supersedes the twelve-per-locale reuse brief at issuecomment-6704762605.

Build the silhouettes and major fracture planes individually from the approved locale concepts. Reuse the successful material swatches, directional mapping tools and render setup. Crystals, mineral plates, crust and ice remain separately editable components. Preserve the hand-drawn outer contour and selected internal marks.

## What is the next concrete review?

Build one bespoke Copper/turquoise asteroid against the original approved Copper concept and the approved Violet execution. Its dense oblong silhouette, broad diagonal cleavage faces and attached copper plates must read in clay. Copper should turn over the fractured forms, with intermittent turquoise along actual contacts. Texture cannot supply a missing fracture or disguise the wrong shape.

Compare the actual model directly with its concept, including a large view, side/rear views and game-scale preview. Present that Copper piece for the owner's visual judgment before expanding into its companions or another bulk set. The target remains five per locale; this first piece establishes the quality for that work.

Non-asteroid debris remains in #1378. Runtime locale routing, production exports, collision updates and merging remain outside the art review pass. Continue in the existing asteroid-locales-art slot; preserve the approved Violet source and the rejected reuse exploration.
