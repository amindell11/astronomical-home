## 5. Path forward

**Open tuning question — `wTerminalField` in dense fields.** A's sweep says 1 over-charges and 0.03–0.1 "looked right" (4 episodes/weight, none clean). B's record says the weight is not the variable: (i) the occupied-corner spike means an endpoint one cell from a rock can pay tens of metres regardless of weight — A's `meanTerminalCost` of 17–50 at w=1 in `dense-sweep.csv` is that mechanism; (ii) the grid slides under the rocks every 0.4 s, so the cost the optimizer chased last solve is not the one it sees now; (iii) with a flat plateau near rocks, raising weight cannot add direction. B's own sweeps at w∈{0.3,1,3} on the *fixed* sampler gave 5/5 transit with zero collisions and w=3 passed 6/6 development rows; the same weights on candidate 1's sampler failed. So the evidence says fix the two sampler defects first, then re-sweep; A's 0.03–0.1 reading is on a sampler that will not survive the fix.
