They explore lower background brightness, saturated shadows, limited cloud highlights, more neutral ship lighting, and a darker planetary night side. They are concepts only, not new engine captures. Planet.shader draws a screen-space sky triangle and uses reference-view coordinates plus camera-position parallax; the planet texture's light and cloud shape are baked. The new nebula materials set _NebulaSpeed to zero. These facts identify the current authoring model; no revised motion model has been implemented or visually validated.

Next rendering study after visual direction is chosen: a distant spherical planet surface with a defined light direction, atmosphere restricted to the illuminated limb, and slowly moving cloud bands.
