Root cause and correction
The previous planet moved about 1.2 pixels during the 120-by-60 world-unit path, while stars and clouds moved much farther. That inverted depth relationship made the planet appear attached to the screen.
The sphere keeps its fixed locale/world center. Its center and radius are scaled closer together about the initial camera position, preserving initial apparent size. Background motion is below planet motion; foreground wisps are above it. No camera-following clamp is added.
The visible surface used only about one tenth of each map's width. New finer cloud maps, surface longitude, restored palettes and a narrower rim restore visible detail without changing ship light.
